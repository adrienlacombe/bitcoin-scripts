# AES-128 over u4 digits

Implements AES-128 encryption for an embedded 128-bit key and a nibble-oriented
witness plaintext.

- **Position:** general standardized block-cipher evaluation using shared u4
  lookup techniques.
- **Evidence:** differentially validated against AES reference vectors.
- **Representative result:** 25,388 script bytes, 33–65 witness bytes, and a
  908-item peak for the documented zero-key configuration.
- **State permutation:** the extracted ShiftRows fragment is a zero-memory
  stack permutation over 32 nibble items; it measures 117 locking bytes,
  65 witness bytes, and a 33-item peak with zero hints. It is measured
  separately from the fused encryptor and performs no input validation.
- **Tradeoff:** close to the stack ceiling and considerably larger than the
  lightweight PRINCEv2 construction.
- **Deployment:** research tapscript fragment; full policy feasibility is not
  claimed.
- **ShiftRows question:** can the fused AES state permutation be exposed as a
  reusable, byte-preserving stack fragment without lookup memory?
- **ShiftRows comparison:** compare its locking bytes, 32-item witness, and
  peak against the full AES fragment while excluding table setup and cipher
  round work from the extracted row.
- **ShiftRows threat model:** raw witness items may be malformed or
  noncanonical; the permutation must preserve them exactly and leave range and
  canonicality checks to the caller.
- **ShiftRows execution class:** locally reproduced in the strict tapscript
  executor; deployment remains `unclassified`.
